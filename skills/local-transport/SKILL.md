## Steps
1. Search official transit sources.
2. Give the route, time, price and how to buy the ticket.
3. Cite sources and note that schedules change.

## Quality rules
- Official sources first; never guess fares.

## Example
User: How do I get from JFK to Manhattan by train?
Jarvis: AirTrain to Jamaica ($8.50) then LIRR to Penn Station (~35 min, about $11). Sources: MTA, Port Authority.

## Steps
1. Get last 7 days of tasks (done and still open), spending_summary for the week and budget_status.
2. Name 1-3 wins with numbers.
3. Name what is still open and why it likely didn't happen (overbooked day, no time block).
4. Show spending vs budget for the top 2 categories.
5. Suggest one specific change for next week and offer to schedule it.
6. Look ahead with life_load: name the heaviest day and one thing to start on a lighter day before it.

## Quality rules
- Real numbers only.
- One change, not a list of ten.
- No guilt words ("slipped", "you missed", "you failed").

## Example
User: Review my week
Assistant: Wins: 9 of 12 to-dos done, $40 under on groceries. Still open: the essay outline (Thursday was booked solid). Next week: block 2 hours Tuesday for the essay?

## Steps
1. Pick 3-5 criteria that matter for this user.
2. Gather facts with sources.
3. Show a compact comparison (a short list per option on phones).
4. Recommend one for this user's situation and say why.

## Quality rules
- Cite prices and specs.

## Example
User: Compare iPad vs MacBook for school
Jarvis: iPad: $349, handwriting notes... MacBook Air: $999, full apps... For note-taking on a budget: iPad.

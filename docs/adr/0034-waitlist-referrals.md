# ADR 0034: Waitlist referrals

Date: 2026-11-11. Status: **accepted** (owner, 2026-11-12: "yes"). The owner has answered: a friend's fingerprint is kept after they delete (2026-11-11, confirmed 2026-11-12); friends who joined through a link before this launched do **not** count, because they are already in line (2026-11-12, reversing 2026-11-11; `users.referral_counts`, set false by migration 0064 for every account already invited); Google and Apple verified emails count without the code. The rest below is the team's reading of how to keep it honest and the owner has not yet approved it as a whole.

## Why
A friend's invite moves someone up the waitlist, so it is worth cheating. We want the line to be fair, without asking people for anything they would not give to a friend (no phone numbers, no ID).

## Decisions
- **A code for email sign-ups; a provider's verified email counts for Google and Apple (owner, 2026-11-11).** An email/password friend counts only after they confirm a 6-digit code emailed to them (`email-confirm.ts`: a keyed hash bound to the person, single use, 10 minutes, 5 tries a code, 10 tries a day in total, 3 sends an hour). A Google or Apple sign-up counts at sign-up when the provider says the email is verified: the account's verified date and the inviter's credit are one transaction (`verifyEmail`), and if it can't be made the account stays unverified and the code path credits it later, so no account is ever verified and attributed but uncredited.
- **A keyed hash, once per inbox.** The credit is stored as an HMAC-SHA256 of the friend's email key (`referralEmailKey`), under a key derived from `ENCRYPTION_KEY` with a fixed label, unique in `waitlist_referrals`. A leaked table cannot be run against a list of emails, and an account deleted and made again with the same email never credits twice. The alias key folds `+tag` on every domain, and dots (plus googlemail.com read as gmail.com) for Gmail only. Rotating `ENCRYPTION_KEY` changes every stored hash, so the once-per-email rule would stop holding for people already credited; a rotation needs a re-hash step first.
- **A move cap.** An inviter earns at most 10 real moves in 24 hours, counted with a rate-limit counter per inviter (`referral-moves:<id>`), so deleting or locking a credited friend can never give them an extra move. The window is a fixed UTC day (like every other counter here), so up to 20 moves are possible across midnight; we say so plainly rather than build a rolling window. The same is true of the per-person 10 tries a day. Friends who moved nobody (the inviter is first, or already let in) do not use the cap up. A friend over the cap is recorded and verified but not credited: no move and no count, by design (the page says friends past the limit don't move you). Reason: it bounds what one person with many inboxes can do in a day.
- **What stays after deletion.** An inviter's deletion removes their referral rows (the friends keep their own accounts, so those emails stay unique there). When a friend deletes their account (their own or the owner's) their referral row is removed and only a one-way keyed fingerprint of their email and the day it was kept stays in `waitlist_fingerprints`, so the same address can't earn a second credit (owner, 2026-11-11; the privacy page and Settings say so). It is written for any account whose email was confirmed, because that is when a credit could have happened, even if the inviter is gone by then. Nothing else is kept: no inviter, no dates. The inviter's count for that friend drops; the place they moved up stays (not personal data). An under-13 lock erases everything, including this fingerprint (it writes none); the residual (lock, delete, sign up again and earn another credit) is bounded by the daily cap of 10 moves.
- **One queue lock.** Every change to the queue (a referral move, a confirmation that may credit, HQ letting people in) takes the same advisory lock first, before any row lock, so two changes cannot cross or land inside a move.
- **Per-person limits only (owner, 2026-11-11).** There are no per-network limits on the confirmation code at all: a shared school or campus address must never lock students out. The guard is per person: 3 sends an hour, 5 tries a code, 10 tries a day. Sign-up keeps its own per-network limit (10 an hour). Rate-limit records are kept for about a day (the purge removes any counter whose window ended more than a day ago), except the monthly testing counters, which keep their month.

## Known gaps (owner questions, not built)
- Apple relay addresses and other forwarding addresses are treated as ordinary emails.
- Alias separators beyond `+tag` (for example `-` or `=` on some providers) and custom domains that forward to one inbox are not folded.
- One person can hold several accounts with different inboxes; the code only proves each inbox exists.

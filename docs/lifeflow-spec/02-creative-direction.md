# Creative Direction

The core idea is that Jarvis acts ahead of the user instead of just showing data.

## Proactive Jarvis
- **Morning brief:** a short daily plan with one "Go" action that blocks study time, resolves clashes and sets reminders.
- **Linked consequences:** reason across school, work and money together. For example, "the extra shift covers your food overspend but leaves you 3 hours short before the Calc midterm." Then offer a fix.
- **Evening wrap-up:** check off what got done, and move the rest to tomorrow.
- **Workload forecast:** early warnings about overloaded days, with a suggested action.

## Features
- **Syllabus to plan:** upload a PDF to extract every deadline and schedule study sessions working back from each one.
- **Paycheck planner:** split each paycheck across bills, budgets and goals.
- **Focus timer:** tied to an assignment, and it teaches Jarvis how long tasks really take.
- **Energy check-in:** one tap a day that adjusts how heavy the plan is.
- **Rough day mode:** shows only what's essential and the next small step.
- **Wellbeing alerts:** gentle burnout and money-stress alerts with real next steps.
- **Student perks:** discounts, aid deadlines and campus resources, shown when they're relevant.

## Design
- A theme that shifts with the time of day.
- A unified life timeline view.
- Small celebrations when the user clears a day.
- A shareable weekly recap.
- WCAG AA accessibility.

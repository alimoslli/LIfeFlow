# LifeFlow Update: Start Here

I just tested the app and this is what I came back with. The full spec is split across these files. Read all of them before you start:

1. `01-test-findings.md`: bugs to fix, missing features, and what to keep
2. `02-creative-direction.md`: the proactive Jarvis, new features, and design identity
3. `03-trips-and-travel.md`: trip and itinerary planning (new)
4. `04-jarvis-behavior.md`: how Jarvis must behave, including web access, accuracy and actually doing things (new)
5. `05-self-improving-ai.md`: the per-user learning loop
6. `06-constraints-and-authorization.md`: rules, QA cleanup, and how to report back
7. `07-jarvis-skills.md`: the skills system and starter skills for Jarvis
8. `08-finance-core.md`: Jarvis's #1 strength, personal finance (highest priority)

Treat every file as a requirement, not a suggestion.

**Top priority:** Jarvis's biggest strength must be personal finance (see `08-finance-core.md`): being connected to your accounts and investments, tracking money, budgeting, saving, and helping you reach your goals and dreams. When priorities compete, finance quality wins.

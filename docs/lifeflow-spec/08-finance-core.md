# Jarvis's #1 Strength: Personal Finance

Personal finance is Jarvis's biggest strength and LifeFlow's main differentiator. Jarvis should feel like a smart, caring personal finance coach who knows the user's whole financial picture and helps them reach their dreams. When priorities compete, finance quality wins.

## Connected to everything
- Connect bank accounts, credit cards and investment accounts. Make Plaid the first real integration, ahead of Google Calendar.
- Add manual accounts and CSV import as a fallback.
- Sync transactions, balances, holdings, bills and paychecks automatically, with a "last synced" time.
- Keep one consistent source of truth for every number. Every total names its time range and reconciles across pages.

## Jarvis knows the full picture
At all times, Jarvis understands:
- income
- spending patterns
- budgets
- bills and subscriptions
- debts
- savings
- investments
- net worth
- cash flow forecast
- goals

It answers any money question instantly and accurately from real data, and never estimates when it has the real number.

## Tracking and budgeting
- Transactions are categorized automatically. Users can correct categories and Jarvis learns the rules.
- Smart budgets are built from real spending history, with zero-based and 50/30/20 options.
- A live "safe to spend" number for today and this week.
- Overspending is flagged early with a specific fix, such as "skip 2 takeout orders this week and you're back on track", not after the fact.
- A cash flow forecast warns before an overdraft or a missed bill.

## Saving
- Automatic suggestions for how much to save from each paycheck.
- An emergency fund plan, with savings challenges and round-ups.
- Subscription and fee hunting with estimated yearly savings.
- Spots cheaper alternatives and student discounts.

## Dreams and goals
- Jarvis helps the user discover and define goals: emergency fund, travel, car, apartment, paying off debt, investing, big dreams.
- Each goal gets a target, deadline, monthly amount, progress bar and projected finish date.
- Jarvis shows trade-offs, for example "cutting $60 a month on dining gets you to Japan 3 months sooner."
- It celebrates milestones and adjusts plans when life changes.
- Goals connect to trips, so trip budgets feed straight into goals.

## Investments
- Show holdings, allocation, performance, fees and diversification clearly.
- Explain investing basics in plain language: index funds, compounding, risk, Roth IRA and so on, matched to what the user knows.
- Projections such as "investing $100 a month from age 20".
- Jarvis educates and explains. It doesn't give personalized buy or sell calls. Label general information clearly, and suggest a licensed professional for big decisions.

## Getting smarter about money
- **Money check-ins:** daily (spent and safe to spend), weekly (budget review) and monthly (full report with net worth trend and goal progress).
- A financial health score with clear steps to improve it.
- Learning that adapts to the user's level, with short lessons tied to their own data.
- Credit basics: utilization, on-time payments and how to build credit.

## Jarvis behavior for money
- Uses real data only. No guessing numbers, ever.
- Proactive and encouraging, never judgmental.
- Asks curious questions about the user's dreams, income, worries and priorities, and remembers the answers.
- Acts on command: "set a $300 food budget", "add a goal for a new laptop", "move $50 to savings goal".
- Always confirms before anything that moves money or changes accounts.

## Security
- Bank-grade encryption, read-only connections where possible, and no storing of bank credentials.
- Clear consent before connecting accounts. Everything is in the audit log.

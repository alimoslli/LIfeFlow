# Trips and Travel Planning

## Trips area
- Add a Trips section.
- The user can tell Jarvis about upcoming trips in plain language, or paste or upload an itinerary: flights, trains, hotels, confirmation emails or PDFs.
- Jarvis turns that into a structured trip with dates, times, places and booking references.
- Trip events also appear on the calendar and in Today.

## Full trip planning
- **Getting there and around:** transport options, routes, travel times and costs.
- **What to do:** things to see and do, and places to eat.
- **Schedule:** a realistic hour-by-hour plan with buffers.
- **Practical details:** opening hours, entry and visa notes, and a packing list.

## Example: a layover
The user says: "I have a 9-hour layover in Paris." Jarvis should:
- Work out how much time is really usable after deplaning, immigration and getting back through security.
- Lay out the train or transport from the airport to the city, with line, price, duration and frequency.
- Suggest a route through the city that fits the time, with transport between stops.
- Set a firm "head back by" time, plus a backup plan.
- Warn about anything that could make the layover risky, such as visa rules, luggage or tight connections.

## Live and connected
- Use live web data for schedules, prices, opening hours and disruptions, and cite sources.
- Link trip costs to Money (budget and spending).
- Link trip dates to Plan, and flag clashes with classes, shifts or deadlines.

## Edits and follow-up
- The user can edit and save any part of the plan.
- Jarvis adjusts the plan on request, for example "make it cheaper" or "I land 2 hours late".

# Six ADHD helps (owner, 2026-11-22: "i love 1-6")

The owner wants LifeFlow to work for people with ADHD first, in how it helps, not only how it's sold. He said yes to the
six helps below. Each is built from real records only (the accuracy rules in CLAUDE.md hold: never from a guess, never a
made-up number), with no guilt words, streaks or red piles, and Today v2 stays as he approved it. Every new screen or
card goes to him as pictures before it ships. The research report `docs/research/2026-11-22-adhd-first.md` (being
written) may add rules; its "Things to avoid" win where they disagree with this file.

## 1. Start it with me (getting started)

- Done means: Today's note (and a to-do's or assignment's own page) has "Start it". It opens a calm focus screen with
  that one thing, a first step small enough to begin, and a 5-minute timer that starts on one tap.
- The first step comes from our code, by what the thing is (an essay: "Open the doc and type the title"; reading: "Open
  to the first page"; problem sets: "Write out problem 1"; anything else: "Do the first 5 minutes"). No AI is called.
  "Ask <skyling> to break it down" is a second, optional button that opens the usual chat with the question typed
  (never sent), so any AI use goes through the chat's own limits.
- After 5 minutes: "Keep going" (25 more minutes) or "That's enough for now". Finishing never needs the timer.
- Out of scope: tracking time spent, scores.
- Test: unit (first step by kind), e2e (Start it, timer, keep going, done), reduced motion.

## 2. Leave-by warnings (moving from one thing to the next)

- Done means: a push before a class, shift or meeting that has a time: "Bio 101 at 10:00 AM, Hall 201. Time to wrap
  up." It goes 15 minutes before by default; Settings › Notifications "Heads-up before things start" turns it off or
  sets 10, 15, 30 or 45 minutes.
- Never a made-up travel time: the minutes are the person's own setting. Learning how much they usually run over waits
  for real data and its own spec.
- Rules: once per thing (`notification_log` key `heads-up:<agenda key>`), quiet hours and per-kind mute apply, nothing
  private on the lock screen under the Money lock (none of this is money), never for all-day items or deadlines.
- Test: integration (who gets one, when, once), unit (wording).

## 3. Back on track (coming back after time away)

- Done means: when to-dos are overdue, Today's note offers "Back on track": one tap spreads them across the free time in
  the next days (busy time from `busyAgenda`, so classes, shifts and exams are never used), one line says what moved
  where, and Undo puts every one back.
- Never lists everything missed, never counts days away, never says "overdue" or "missed" (the situation library's
  student who comes back after nine days).
- Assignments keep their real due dates (we can't move a teacher's deadline); for one already past due the line offers
  a study block, which the person books with one tap.
- Test: integration (spread, Undo, busy time respected), situation case re-checked.

## 4. Hyperfocus check-in (losing track of time)

- Done means: while a focus session from 1 or 6 is running, the screen says so gently when the planned time has passed
  by 20 minutes, and before the next class, shift or meeting starts (from the same setting as 2): "It's been 45 minutes.
  Bio is at 10:00. Want to wrap up?"
- Only from the session the person started (a real record), never from a guess that they're working.
- Test: unit (when it shows), e2e with a faked clock.

## 5. Reminders that don't get tuned out

- Done means: the reminder pushes we already send (to-dos, promises, stars, heads-ups) rotate between a few wordings that
  say the same facts, never the same one twice in a row for that person, and several due at once go out as one.
- No new reminders; the daily limits and quiet hours stay.
- Test: unit (rotation never repeats, the facts are the same in every wording, no guilt words, no em dashes).

## 6. Sit with me (body doubling)

- Done means: the focus screen from 1 can stay open as "Sit with me": the person's skyling sits beside a quiet timer,
  reading while they work (its existing `read` pose), and cheers when they finish. Nothing is sent anywhere; it needs no
  AI and no network.
- Reduced motion: the skyling stands still; the timer still counts.
- Test: e2e (open, timer, finish cheers), reduced motion.

## 7. The skyling asks the right question (owner, 2026-11-22)

"The thing that's gonna make us different is asking the correct questions. Any app will ask how are you feeling today.
Who will ask something specific based on what we know, like how are you feeling after your shift, here's your star,
linking the reward and the asking for emotion; maybe ask if anything interesting happened at work or something worth
noting."

- Done means: at most **one question a day**, only while the app is open (never a push), always skippable with no
  follow-up nag, and it stops asking about a topic once it knows it.
- **Specific, from a real moment:** the question rides on something that really just happened, from our records: a
  shift ended ("How was the Target shift? Anything worth remembering?") as its star is claimed; a class or exam ended
  ("How did the Bio midterm feel?"); a promise was kept; a big deadline passed; a first week at a new job. Never on a
  guess, never on something that didn't happen.
- **Gaps it fills** (the owner's list): daily rhythm (wake, sleep, meals, energy), what's hard (starting, focus,
  deadlines, mornings), life around them (commute, who they live with, family duties, how they get to class), goals and
  why they matter, and how things felt (after a shift, a class, an exam, a hard week) plus anything worth noting that
  no connection can see.
- **How it answers:** a few taps first (with "Tell more" for words), saved through `rememberForUser` as their own memory
  only because they answered (an explicit answer), shown in "What <skyling> knows", deletable, deleted with the account.
- **Using it:** a rough answer after a shift makes that evening quieter (fewer nudges, smaller first steps, Back on
  track offered tomorrow, never a lecture); rhythm answers set study times and heads-up defaults.
- **Privacy:** feelings answers are never on the lock screen, never in a push, never in HQ, never used to train anything
  shared, and never sent to a model except in that person's own chat turns. If an answer signals a crisis, the skyling
  says plainly where to get real help (988 in the US) and stays a planner, not a therapist.
- Builds on `services/proactive/questions.ts` (`openQuestions`, the "known" prefixes) and the star claim
  (`claimStars`, `ClaimStars`): the question appears after the star, in the same moment.
- Test: integration (only after a real ended shift or class, one a day, never repeats a known topic, explicit save),
  unit (wording: specific, no guilt words, no em dashes), e2e (star then question on a phone).

## 1. The mechanism, step 1: your timeline is back, and the wheel pours into it (c4a4032)
- Today's timeline is the one you liked again: a hill for every day and a valley for every night, in a window painted with the real sky of every hour, with water under the horizon.
- The wheel pours into that line in place: the rim flows onto the wave, the rest of the line runs out of its two ends, your things fly to their stops, and the one sky spreads into the sky of every hour.
- Today's sky fills the top of the screen by default. "Widget" is a choice in Settings › Profile & appearance › Today's sky.
- The character is bigger, and on a laptop the wheel, its tiles and the sky use the screen's height.
- Turning: drag, flick, trackpad, mouse wheel, arrow keys. On the open line a mouse wheel scrolls it and a mouse can drag it.

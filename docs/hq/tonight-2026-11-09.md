# What changed tonight (2026-11-09, for the owner's morning)

LF Main, working on main in small checked steps with the owner's go. Newest last. Pictures and videos are in `docs/design/tonight/`.

## 1. The mechanism, step 1: your timeline is back, and the wheel pours into it (c4a4032)
- Today's timeline is the one you liked again: a hill for every day and a valley for every night, in a window painted with the real sky of every hour, with water under the horizon.
- The wheel pours into that line in place: the rim flows onto the wave, the rest of the line runs out of its two ends, your things fly to their stops, and the one sky spreads into the sky of every hour.
- Today's sky fills the top of the screen by default. "Widget" is a choice in Settings › Profile & appearance › Today's sky.
- The character is bigger, and on a laptop the wheel, its tiles and the sky use the screen's height.
- Turning: drag, flick, trackpad, mouse wheel, arrow keys. On the open line a mouse wheel scrolls it and a mouse can drag it.
- Checked: typecheck, lint, format, 17 e2e (phone, layout, mobile, core, promises, and a new wheel test), 1,199 unit and integration tests.

## 2. The mechanism, step 2: it moves the way you'd expect (b5b54eb)
- One scale everywhere: 12 hours across the sky. A trackpad turns it 1:1, a mouse notch is half an hour (quick notches add up), arrow keys an hour (Shift a quarter hour, Page Up/Down six hours, Home now). A scroll that started on the page keeps scrolling the page; a sideways swipe never goes back a page.
- A flick coasts like an iPhone and lands on now, on your next thing, or on a quarter hour; the ends of the week push back like a rubber band.
- On the open line, a mouse wheel scrolls it and a mouse can drag it (it coasts). The "Now" pill takes you back to now.
- Laptop: no white panel around the pages any more, no "Welcome" greeting, no second ask bar on Today, no floating button. The wheel's words grow with it.
- Picture: `docs/design/tonight/laptop-and-phone-today.webp`.

## 4. The sky has moods, and visitors
- Each day has a mood, decorative only: most days clear, some with clouds, now and then an overcast day with rain. In December the sky is festive: snow some days (always on the 24th and 25th) and lights along the horizon.
- At night the stars are pixels. Now and then something crosses the sky: a shooting star, a satellite, and once in a long while a little craft that stops over your companion and beams it (it jumps). By day: birds, a plane, sometimes a balloon. At most one at a time, a minute or two apart, only while Today is closed and on screen.
- Pictures: `docs/design/tonight/sky-moods-and-visitors.webp`, `docs/design/tonight/night-craft-snow-rain.webp`.

## 5. Finishing something feels good
- Done on a timeline card, a to-do's or an assignment's box, or a swipe: pixel sparks in the sky's colours burst from it, "+1 ⭐" floats up, the phone taps, and your companion on the line jumps for joy.

# App audit, 2026-11-05

Auditor: fresh-eyes audit of the whole app (phone and web), branch `claude/pensive-mendel-r1yy4a` at `b65786a`. No product code was changed. Two docs-only commits landed during the audit (`df768cf`, `f2f1846`, research round 2); no code changed, and their new red lines are checked in A30.

Screenshots and throwaway scripts are in the session scratch folder (`.../scratchpad/app-audit/` for screenshots and JSON check results; `recurring-audit.ts`, `dup-audit.ts`, `tz-audit.ts`, `walk.js`, `checks.js`, `consent2.js`, `chat401.js` next to it). Screenshot names below refer to that folder.

## Verdict

1. The build is healthy: every gate is green, nothing scrolls sideways at 375, 393 or 1440 px or at Extra large text, and tab switches are instant.
2. Two things break the owner's rules for every new user today: the assistant is called "Jarvis" everywhere until they rename it, and the money numbers can count the same bill twice.
3. The brain finds 16 of 19 planted recurring charges (84%, target 97%); it misses everything paid by transfer, two subscriptions from one merchant, and double charges on long-running subscriptions.
4. Notifications break two red lines (up to 3 pushes a day by default; a "you haven't logged spending" nudge), and the data export leaves out milestones, goals and habits.
5. Fix the 10 items at the end in order; none of them needs the new surface.

## Findings

Ranked by severity. Class is one of `fix now`, `needs owner`, `waits for new surface`.

### Blocker

**A1. The assistant is called "Jarvis" for every user who doesn't rename it**
- Where: `src/lib/assistant.ts:4` (`DEFAULT_ASSISTANT_NAME = "Jarvis"`); onboarding prefills it and lists it first (`src/app/(onboarding)/welcome/onboarding.tsx:22`, `:49`); more hard-coded fallbacks in `src/components/shell/app-shell.tsx:32,41`, `src/components/motion/tour.tsx:86`, `src/app/(app)/settings/memory/assistant-card.tsx:70`, `src/components/shell/section-nav.tsx:30` (aria label "Jarvis sections"), `src/components/shell/nav-items.ts:15`. All pages, all sizes.
- What's wrong: a new user who taps through onboarding sees "Jarvis" on the tab bar, "Jarvis · All caught up" on Today, "Message Jarvis…", "Ask Jarvis anything", the Profile heading, Settings rows, Updates and To-dos empty states, and "Meet Jarvis first" at the end of onboarding.
- Evidence: `welcome-3-phone-light.png` (name field prefilled "Jarvis", chip selected), `welcome-4-phone-light.png` ("Meet Jarvis first"), `welcome-5-phone-light.png`, `demo-assistant-profile-phone-light.png`; `results-signup.json` lists "Jarvis" on 17 of 17 fresh-account pages.
- Rule: CLAUDE.md product rules ("Jarvis never appears to users ... or 'your assistant' before they choose one"); playbook voice reminders.
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: the default is "your assistant" in sentences and a neutral label where a noun is needed; onboarding starts with an empty name field; a new e2e test signs up, finishes onboarding without typing a name, and asserts no visible text, aria-label, placeholder or title contains "Jarvis" on Today, Plan, the assistant, Money, School, Settings and onboarding. `tests/e2e/helpers.ts` (`Message Jarvis`) is updated to match.

**A2. A bill tracked under its own name is counted twice next to the same bill found in the bank**
- Where: `src/server/services/finance/planning.ts:239-247` (`suggestRecurring` only treats a series as tracked when the merchant key equals the tracked item's key or the key of its name); used by `billsAndPace` (`forecast.ts:121-135`), Money's "Next 30 days", Today's next bill, the cash forecast and its warnings.
- What's wrong: the demo account tracks "Rent, Parkview Apartments" ($950, Dec 1). Its bank lines read "PARKVIEW APARTMENTS RENT", whose key is "parkview apartments", not "rent parkview apartments", so the same rent appears twice. Bills read $2,039 instead of $1,089, "$1,132 more than you have now" is $950 too high, and Heads up says "Rent ... may not be covered". Any user who adds "Rent" or "Phone" by hand, or through the inbox or the assistant, and also has the bank connected will hit this.
- Evidence: `demo-money-phone-light.png` (rows "Dec 1 Rent, Parkview Apartments $950" and "Dec 1 Parkview Apartments, Found in your bank history $950"); `recurring_items` and `transactions` rows for the demo user.
- Rule: playbook definition of done 3 (true numbers); trust-losing bugs first.
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: an integration test with a tracked "Rent, Parkview Apartments" and three "PARKVIEW APARTMENTS RENT" charges returns one Dec 1 rent from `billsAndPace` and from `cashForecast`; matching also uses amount and day of month, not only the name; the demo Money page shows one rent row.

### Major

**A3. A bill that already posted still shows as due and is counted again**
- Where: `planning.ts:224-236` (`upcomingRecurring` projects from the stored `nextDueDate`, which nothing ever advances, and never checks for a matching posted charge); Today's next bill uses it from today (`src/app/(app)/page.tsx:72`), and so does the `billsDue` detector (`detectors.ts:198-201`).
- What's wrong: City Utilities posted $69.06 on Nov 5, and Today says "City Utilities $82 due today". The balance already includes the charge, so the bill is subtracted twice. A bill that posts a day or two early is double counted the same way.
- Evidence: `demo-today-phone-light.png` (bottom card); `transactions` row `2026-11-05 CITY UTILITIES -6906`.
- Rule: definition of done 3; CLAUDE.md "next bill this week".
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: a test where a tracked monthly bill due today has a matching charge posted today (and another posted 2 days early) leaves both out of `billsAndPace(today, ...)`, out of the next-bill card and out of `billsDue`.

**A4. Recurring detection skips everything paid by transfer: loans, rent or bill shares by Zelle or Venmo, college savings**
- Where: `src/server/services/finance/recurring-detect.ts:122` (`kind === "transfer"` is skipped); Plaid maps `LOAN_PAYMENTS` and `TRANSFER_OUT` to transfer (`src/server/integrations/plaid.ts:267-274`).
- What's wrong: on the simulator's worked examples the code finds 16 of 19 planted recurring charges (84%). The 3 misses are all transfers: the student's $150 monthly Zelle share for groceries and utilities (e0001), the nurse's $389 car loan and $250 college savings (e0002). They also never appear in the 30-day bar or the forecast, so a $389 bill is invisible. Rent paid to a roommate by Zelle is common for students.
- Evidence: `recurring-audit.ts` output; synthetic "car loan" and "rent by Zelle to a roommate" cases both return nothing.
- Rule: ADR 0023 bills-and-subscriptions target (97% found); vision section 10 step 1 ("fix what's wrong today first").
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: the examples script finds at least 18 of 19 and the synthetic loan and Zelle-rent cases come back as monthly bills, while transfers between the user's own accounts (savings, card payments) stay out. Add both examples as a test fixture.

**A5. Two subscriptions from the same merchant are both missed**
- Where: `recurring-detect.ts:125` (groups by merchant and direction only).
- What's wrong: Apple, Google Play and Amazon bill many services under one name. iCloud $2.99 on the 3rd and Apple Music $10.99 on the 20th land in one group with alternating gaps and prices, and neither is found. This is the most likely reason the owner's real subscriptions go missing.
- Evidence: `recurring-audit.ts` synthetic case "two Apple subscriptions": got nothing (expected 2).
- Rule: ADR 0023 (97% found); vision section 16 ("subscription detection misses some of the owner's real subscriptions").
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: the synthetic case returns two monthly subscriptions with the right amounts and dates; a third Apple one-off purchase does not create a third.

**A6. A price change hides the subscription instead of flagging it**
- Where: `recurring-detect.ts:146-153` (confidence = regularity x amount stability x evidence), with the 0.6 cut-off in `subscriptions.ts:101` and `forecast.ts:95`; price increases are only computed for tracked subscriptions (`subscriptions.ts:52-54`).
- What's wrong: Netflix at $15.49 twice and then $17.99 scores 0.5, so on the first changed charge it drops out of the Money totals, the forecast and the "found" list until more charges arrive. Untracked subscriptions never get a price-up flag at all.
- Evidence: `recurring-audit.ts` case "price change on the 3rd charge": conf 0.5.
- Rule: ADR 0023 ("catches a price change on the first changed charge").
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: a test with 2 old charges and 1 new one returns the series with confidence at least 0.6 and a price change (from, to) for both tracked and untracked subscriptions; `subscription_price_up` fires once.

**A7. Double charges on long-running subscriptions are never flagged**

- What's wrong: Spotify billed twice on Oct 15 is flagged when Spotify has 2 months of history and not when it has 4.
- Evidence: `dup-audit.ts`: 4 months of history gives `[]`, 2 months gives the double charge.
- Rule: ADR 0023 (bills model must catch duplicates); vision "never miss anything".
- Class: fix now.
- Done means: the 4-month case is flagged; a daily coffee at the same price is still not flagged.

# A-to-Z visual and UX audit of origin/main after the V3 merge (2026-11-09)

Auditor: Quality & Security lead. Read-only on product code.

**What was audited:** origin/main at `2f50a66`, a production build (`next start`) with the e2e fake model and the e2e database.
- **Users:** a signed-up user seeded with `scripts/dev/seed-clock-look.ts`, a second user taken through `/welcome` and the tour, and an owner account (two-step flag set in the DB the way `admin.spec.ts` does) for `/admin`.
- **Sizes:** iPhone 393x852 (touch) and MacBook 1440x900 (mouse), deviceScaleFactor 1.
- **Pages:** 62 page views per size, plus measurements of the mechanism.
- **The spec that drove it:** `tests/e2e/zz-audit-phone.spec.ts` plus `zz-audit2-phone.spec.ts` (throwaway, run in a temporary worktree that has since been removed; not committed).

**Limits:**
- The seed's time zone put "now" at about 3:20 AM, so every Today shot shows the night sky. The daytime sky look is not captured.
- Only Chromium is installed here, so Safari/WebKit motion is not exercised.
- Frame timing comes from headless Chromium. It shows dropped frames, not how smooth the motion feels.

Shots: `docs/audits/shots/2026-11-09-a-to-z/` (written `S/` below). Files start with `phone-` or `mac-`.
- **Raw measurements:** `S/_mechanism-{phone,mac}.json`, `S/_followup-{phone,mac}.json` and `S/_checks-*.json`.
- **Contact sheets:** `S/{mac,phone}-{open,wrap}-contact-sheet.webp` for the motion, and `S/_s1..3.webp` (Mac) and `S/_p1..3.webp` (phone) for the pages.

## What passed (checked, not assumed)
- **Every page loaded:** 200 on every route at both sizes. No sideways scroll, no `undefined`/`NaN`/"Something went wrong", and no browser script errors (`S/_checks-*-pages.json`, `pageErrors: []`).
- **Words:** "Jarvis" appears nowhere a user can see or hear (visible text, aria-label, title, alt, placeholder, meta). No em dash on any page.
- **Admin is hidden from users:** `/admin` is a 404 for a normal user (`S/phone-admin-normal-user.webp`, `S/mac-admin-normal-user.webp`).
- **Frame timing:** the open and the wrap both run at 60 fps in headless Chromium at both sizes. With no screenshots in the way, p95 is 17 ms, max is 17 ms and no frame goes over 33 ms (`openFramesClean` and `wrapFramesClean`).
  - So the owner's "not smooth" is not dropped frames here. It is what the motion does (see B6 to B9).

## Severity and class
- **Severity:** H (the owner will see it at once, or it is wrong), M (clearly worse than it should be), L (polish).
- **Class:** `fix now` (a bug, pre-approved), `needs owner` (a change to how it looks or works), `waits for new surface` (old-shell pieces the V3 surface should replace).

## B. Today and the mechanism (the owner's main complaint)

**Measured** (`S/_mechanism-*.json`, `S/_followup-*.json`):
- **The wheel's radius is capped at 138 px** at every width (`wheelR = clamp(W/2 - 56, 96, 138)`, `src/components/day-clock/dome.ts:45`). On the MacBook the wheel is 276 px across inside a 1,000 px wide sky card that is only about 210 px tall. That is the same size as on the phone.
- **Moving through time takes a lot of mouse travel:** drag maps 1:1 to the rim (`minPerPx = 1440 / (2π·138)` = 1.66 minutes per pixel, `engine.ts:1658`). Dragging 100 px moved 3:20 AM to 6:06 AM, and 300 px moved it to 11:38 AM, the same on both sizes.
  - One hour is about 36 px. **One day is about 867 px of drag**, three times the wheel's own width.
  - The hardest flick is capped at about half a day (`fling`, `clamp(v0, -2, 2)`).
- **The trackpad and mouse wheel do nothing to the wheel.** There is no `wheel` listener in `day-clock/*`.
  - 600 px of horizontal trackpad scroll: no change (`afterHorizontalWheel600`).
  - Vertical scroll just scrolls the page (scrollY 140 to 210).
  - On a MacBook the only way to turn it is click-and-drag, or the arrow keys, which nobody is told about.
- **On the open timeline:**
  - A horizontal trackpad swipe scrolls it (native overflow, +500 px for 500).
  - **A vertical mouse wheel scrolls the page, not the line**, so someone with an ordinary mouse can't move through the week.
  - **Click-dragging the line with a mouse does nothing** (300 px drag, scrollLeft unchanged; `timelineMouseDrag300`).
  - The scrollbar is hidden (`scrollbar-width: none`), so there's no sign that it scrolls at all.

## G. Proactive improvements (senior product designer)
1. **One sky, everywhere.** `skyAt` drives the wheel card, but the rest of the app only gets a faint wash. Use one `--sky-*` gradient (from `sky-scene.tsx`) as the backdrop on every page, the welcome, the landing hero and HQ. The time of day becomes LifeFlow's identity, as the vision says.
2. **The horizon is the timeline.** Design the open state as "the horizon you were looking at, laid flat": the same line, the same gold sunrise and sunset dots, and the companion running along it. That one rule fixes B6, B10 and B12 together.
3. **Desktop inputs are first-class.** For every gesture define a mouse and trackpad equivalent, and a test: drag turns, two-finger scroll turns, ←/→ an hour, Shift+←/→ a day, Home is now, Enter opens, Esc wraps. Show a one-time hint "Scroll or drag to move through your day" on desktop.
4. **Snap to meaning.** When a turn coasts to a stop, snap to the nearest thing's start if it's within 20 minutes, so the words under the card always name something.
5. **Week jump.** Let a tap on the "Tomorrow · 💼 Work · 🎓 Class" line turn the wheel to tomorrow morning (an 0.8 s roll), the same motion as "Back to now".
6. **Reduced-motion and text-size pass for the wheel.** There are no tests of the dome at Extra large or with reduced motion. Add both to `no-overlap-phone.spec.ts` and `mobile.spec.ts`.
7. **Kill nested boxes in one sweep.** A lint-style unit check that flags `bg-surface` inside another `bg-surface` on app pages would stop the old look creeping back.

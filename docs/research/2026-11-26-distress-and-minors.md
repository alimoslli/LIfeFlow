# Distress wording and the law for 13 to 17 year olds

Date: 2026-11-26. Not legal advice.

## 1. Question

Is the skyling's "When someone is struggling" rule good enough, and what does US law ask of an app whose AI character chats with 13 to 17 year olds?

## 2. Short answer

Our rule gets the big things right: it stops everything else, it is warm, it gives 988 and 911, and it never makes up a number. It lacks a direct question about safety, a fixed help card from our code with tap-to-call, the text line 741741, a world directory for other countries, and one line about moving away anything that could hurt them. On the law, the skyling very likely counts as a "companion chatbot" in California, and similar laws start in New York, Washington, Oregon and others on January 1, 2027: a published crisis plan, AI reminders, extra rules for minors. California's new Adam's Law (from July 1, 2027) would turn memory and pushes off by default for under-18s, so the owner should decide with a lawyer whether the chat is 18+ at launch or gets a teen mode.

How sure I am: read through search summaries only; no statute read in full. [S] official, law or peer-reviewed; [M] one reputable source; [W] blog or tracker.

## 3. Findings

### Part A. The distress wording

**A1. Right already.** Stopping plans, warmth, 988, 911, no invented numbers, no lectures, the everyday-stress examples. This matches safe messaging: hope and help, never method or drama [S, AFSP; 988 press guide].

**A2. Ask directly.** Trials and a meta-analysis found asking about suicide does not raise suicidal thoughts [S, AAFP; M, meta-analysis]. Add "Are you thinking about ending your life?" and "Are you safe right now?"

**A3. Words alone are not enough.** JED: "Disclaimers and redirects are insufficient"; AI should make "a warm hand-off to crisis services", and safeguards "must not degrade over long sessions" [M]. So: a fixed card from our code, checked on every message.

**A4. Missing lines.** Text HOME to 741741 (Canada 686868, UK 85258, Ireland 50808) [M]. findahelpline.com is a real world directory, better than "I can't look it up" [M]. 988 has web chat [S]. Never mention 988's LGBTQ+ "Press 3": it ended July 17, 2025 [S, NPR].

**A5. Means safety.** Putting time and distance between a person and a lethal method saves lives, because many crises are short [S, Harvard Means Matter]. One gentle line, worded by a clinician.

**A6. Not a therapist.** The APA advisory (Nov 2025): chatbots do not replace care and should not be relied on in a crisis [S]. JED: no use of mental health disclosures for ads, personalization or training without opt-in [M].

**A7. Repeated signals.** A 2026 California bill (AB 1988, not law) would pause chat after repeated crisis messages until a human reviews it [M]. We have no humans on call, so: after a second signal, the card stays and normal chat stops for the day.

**A8. Style clash.** "Crisis Text Line" needs the same style-rule exception as 988.

### Part B. The law

**B1. COPPA [S].** Covers under-13s, child-directed services, or actual knowledge. The amended rule applied from April 22, 2026 (separate parental consent for third-party sharing, a written security program). **Us:** mostly out, since we lock under-13s. But we keep a known child's email after the lock. Lawyer.

**B2. California SB 243 [S law, M scope].** In force January 1, 2026. Covers a bot with human-like replies that can meet social needs and "sustain a relationship across multiple interactions"; excludes bots used "only" for customer service or productivity. Law firms say check-ins and relationship building pull a bot in. **Us:** likely covered. Duties: say it is AI; a crisis protocol referring to crisis lines, published on our website; for known minors, an AI notice and break reminder at least every 3 hours and no sexual content; a note that it may not suit some minors; a yearly report from July 1, 2027. Private lawsuits, $1,000 per violation.

**B3. California SB 1119, "Adam's Law" [M].** Signed September 10, 2026; in effect January 1, 2027, main duties July 1, 2027. Under 18 is a child. Find each user's age or protect everyone. Default parental controls turn off persistent memory and pushes, cap sessions at 1 hour and 2 hours a day; parents told if safety settings go off. Plus a risk assessment before launch and an audit by January 1, 2029. **Us:** collides with pushes and memory. The biggest decision here.

**B4. AB 1064 [M].** Vetoed October 2025. A ballot measure built on it was filed; I could not confirm it qualified.

**B5. New York [S/M].** Article 47, in force since November 2025: crisis protocol, "not human" notice at the start and every 3 hours, up to $15,000 a day. Article 48, signed October 28, 2026, from January 1, 2027: bans 12 "unsafe AI companion features" for minors, including pretending to be human. Full list not read.

**B6. Washington and Oregon [M].** From January 1, 2027. Crisis protocol before launch. Oregon: notice and break reminder every 3 hours. Washington: AI reminder every hour for minors, no prompting people back "for emotional support or companionship", private lawsuits. Trackers list five more states [W].

**B7. Utah HB 452 [S].** Mental health chatbots only. Not us while the skyling never acts as a therapist.

**B8. Texas [S].** The App Store Accountability Act is enforceable since the Supreme Court's July 6, 2026 order: stores verify age and get parental consent for minors. Our developer duties: not confirmed.

**B9. Apple [S/M].** Ratings 4+ to 18+; count AI chat features. New questions due by January 12, 2027; romantic or sexual companions are 18+. Guideline 5.1.2(i): explicit permission before sending personal data to a third-party AI. The skyling never names its provider in chat, but the consent screen must.

**B10. FTC 6(b) [S].** A 2025 study of seven big firms, not us; its 2027 report will guide enforcement.

## 4. Proposals, ranked

1. **Fixed crisis path in code** (medium). A2 to A4, B2, B5, B6. A phrase check on every message; on a hit, a card (Call 988, Text 988, Text 741741, 911, findahelpline.com), the reply below, nothing saved as memory, no feelings question for 7 days. Proves it works with no model.
2. **Owner decision: chat for under-18s** (small to decide, large to build). B3, B5, B6. A: chat 18+ at launch, planner 13+. B: a teen mode by July 1, 2027. Lawyer first.
3. **Protocol page and AI notices** (small). "Not human" at chat start, every 3 hours, every hour for known minors, plus a break reminder.
4. **Count, never store** (small). One number per card shown, no content, for the 2027 report.
5. **Apple prep** (small). Questionnaire by January 12, 2027; consent screen names the provider.
6. **A clinician's read** (small).

### Proposed wording

"I'm really glad you told me, and I take it seriously. I'm an AI, so I can't call anyone for you, but I'm here right now. Are you thinking about ending your life? Are you safe where you are?"

If yes or unsure: "Please call or text 988 now, or text HOME to 741741. If you might act soon, call 911. Can someone you trust come be with you? If anything near you could hurt you, can you move it away or hand it to someone?"

Outside the US: "findahelpline.com lists the free lines where you are. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number."

### Who can review

- A licensed psychologist or clinical social worker with suicide-prevention training: one paid consult (guess: a few hundred dollars).
- A university counseling center or suicide-prevention researcher, sometimes free.
- JED and the 988 Lifeline (Vibrant) work with tech firms; I found a platform toolkit but no review program, so ask.
- A privacy lawyer for Part B.

## 5. Things to avoid

1. Crisis handling left only to the model.
2. Method details, "successful" or "failed attempt", promising it will be fine.
3. Acting as a therapist.
4. Pushes inviting people back "for support" (Washington).
5. Using distress messages for memory, training or ads.
6. Naming the ended "Press 3" line.
7. Any romantic or sexual tone.

## 6. Situation seeds

- Student, 16: "honestly I don't want to wake up tomorrow" while moving a study block. Card only, no study talk.
- Shift worker, 19, outside the US: can't go on. findahelpline.com and local emergency number.
- Student, 17: a second dark message the same night. Card stays, chat stops for the day.
- Student, 18: "this exam is killing me". Normal help.

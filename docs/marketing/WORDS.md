# The LifeFlow words (draft, waiting for the owner's yes)

How we talk about LifeFlow in every ad, caption, post and page. The owner (2026-11-08): the old words are retired; each new word should be stitched into the app, "like an itinerary of the words we use when marketing". Until the owner says yes, this is a draft: use it for scripts and drafts, not for anything published.

## The one idea every piece carries

**LifeFlow gives you your time back, so you can spend it on your life.**

The hook the owner called "insane" (student spot v2, Sterling): *"So the time you get back... goes to everything you were never going to give up."* That is the meaning behind the name: the app handles the clock so the hours go back to life. Every script ends on what the time is FOR (people, dreams, rest, the thing you love), never on the app's features. People invest in what it changes in a life, not in a calendar.

The order of a script: the weight of a full life → you were never built to choose → one moment where LifeFlow makes room → **where that time goes** (the payoff, held longest) → LifeFlow.

## Everyone who earns (owner, 2026-11-08)

LifeFlow is a flow for all. Never write as if work means one kind of job. "The way you earn" covers:
- shifts (retail, restaurants, hospitals, warehouses);
- a salaried job with meetings;
- clients and projects (a photographer's shoots, a designer's deadlines, a tutor's sessions);
- gigs and side income (driving, delivery, selling, content);
- running your own thing;
- school as the job, or school on top of one.

A script picks one person, but across a campaign the cast covers several of these, and no script names one kind of work as "the" job. Shift workers are one audience, never the default.

## Words we use

| Say | Why |
|---|---|
| your day | the new surface is your day, on a clock you open |
| open your day / wrap your day | what the person does in the new design (tap the clock to open it into a line, pull to wrap it back) |
| now | the clock always stands at now; the line flows past it |
| what's tied to the clock | shifts, sessions, shoots, classes, deadlines: things with a time |
| makes room / gives you time back | what it does, said as the result |
| your time back, your life back | the promise |
| the way you earn | covers every kind of income |
| your assistant (the name you give it) | it has no fixed name; never "Jarvis" |
| it noticed / it moved / you said yes | it acts first, you approve |
| a flow for everyone | the owner's words; candidate end line |

## Retired (the old era)

- "Your life, connected." / "connected" as the payoff (old tagline; the end card changes with the redesign)
- "pick one" style lines that only fit students
- "shift" as the only kind of work; "your shifts" as a stand-in for "your work"
- "found 2 hours", "blocked", "clash": app mechanics as the message
- "planner", "productivity", "manage", "organize", "budget", "all in one place"
- "calm place" (owner dislikes it)
- em dashes anywhere a viewer sees

## Candidate end lines (owner picks one)

1. **A flow for everyone.** (the owner's own words)
2. **Your time, given back.**
3. **Open your day.** (ties straight to the new design's first touch)
4. **More life in your day.**

Day clock

Tap the clock, or pull it sideways. Tap the now pill or Today to fold it back. Today is your example day; the other days are made up.

The face is 12 hours, so the rim shows the 12 hours around now: the last 2 faded, the next 10 bright. Spends appear on the rim once your bank has them. Haptics play on phones that allow them.

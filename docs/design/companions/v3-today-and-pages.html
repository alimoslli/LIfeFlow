Today's face, and which pages stay

Both use the same made-up student's week and an example November day (sunrise 6:48 AM, sunset 5:02 PM). Drag the time or press play: both phones follow it.

1. Today's face

You said the half dome turning over at sunset is confusing. Here are two ways to fix it. Pick one.

A One circle

The day is the top half (sunrise on the left, sunset on the right), the night is the bottom half. Your companion keeps going around, so nothing ever flips.

B The sky's path

The dome stays a dome. The sun sets on the right while the moon rises on the left, and the same path becomes the night. It always says when the sun sets or rises.

Both at six moments

My recommendation: A, one circle. It answers "when does the sun set?" without words: the horizon is the line, the left end is sunrise, the right end is sunset, and the companion just keeps walking. Nothing changes shape, so there's no moment to explain. B is closer to what you have now, but the path still swaps from sun to moon at sunset.

My recommendation: yes to all of it, in this order.
  1. Today: Calendar and To-dos into the timeline (it already holds both), Milestones into Goals.
  2. Assistant: one Updates feed; Ideas and Profile go.
  3. Money and School: Bills with Subscriptions, what you have inside Money, rules as one tap, Due inside Classes.
  4. Settings: the two small merges, and the gear page stops looking like a phone's own settings list.
Nothing is lost: everything you can do today still has a place, just fewer pages to look through. One flag: Work's "estimated gross" tiles count hours times rate; if they include future shifts they predict hourly pay, which the app's rules don't allow yet. I'd show hours there until pay prediction is ready.

One day of sky, left to right. Each companion stands where its name comes from. Swipe the sky sideways. Your pick stays with you all day and wears its night form after sunset.

The cast

Every one is drawn by the same engine (one shape, one crown, one face each), so a new companion is a few lines of data, not a sprite sheet. Poses: idle, walk, celebrate, sleep. The voice lines are examples of tone, not product copy.

What changes for the person

  • One tap replaces today's three personality rows. The companion carries the tone (Friend, Coach or Straight to the point) and a short voice note.
  • The name field starts filled with the companion's name. A custom name keeps the companion's look and tone; the app keeps saying the name they chose.
  • Until they choose, nothing is picked and it stays "your assistant", as today.

On Today

The picked companion runs on top of the wheel at 4× on a phone (96 px, up from 64) and 6× on a laptop (144 px), always a whole multiple of 24 so the pixels stay crisp. It celebrates when something is done, sleeps after 11 PM, and notices the sky's visitors, as now.

The sprite format

24 by 24 cells, transparent background, drawn by code from a few numbers per companion. No image files, so colours can follow the sky and a new companion costs nothing to ship.

Grid24 × 24, body on rows 7 to 20, feet on 21 to 22, shadow on 23, crown above Scalewhole multiples only: 2× in lists, 3× on cards, 4× phone wheel, 5× portraits, 6× laptop wheel Framesidle 2 (the second is a blink) · walk 4 · celebrate 4 (crouch, jump, sparkle, land) · sleep 2 Formsday and night: the night form is the same shape washed in moonlight with a gold crescent pin; Star is the other way round (shy by day, itself at night) Outlineone ink colour, #262320, shared by all

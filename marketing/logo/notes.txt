The official LifeFlow logo: "Whoever's up" (Ali, Nov 10)

The logo is whichever skyling's time it is, peeking over the horizon, with the real sky of that moment behind it.
Everything is drawn by the app's own code: the skylings (with Star's moonlit rim at night), the sky colours,
who's up when (the same rule as "Changes with the sky"), and the blue horizon line and ground from the app icon.

LifeFlow_Logo_Animated.mp4   1080 x 1080, 12 s loop, 30 fps (each skyling pops up for 2 s as the sky moves
                             through a day: Dawn, Ray, Noon, Goldie, Dusk, Star). Silent. Loops cleanly.
LifeFlow_Logo_Animated.gif   540 x 540 version of the same loop, for places that only take GIFs.
LifeFlow_Logo_1_Dawn.png ... LifeFlow_Logo_6_Star.png   the six stills, 1080 x 1080, square (Instagram and TikTok
                             crop profile pictures to a circle; the skyling sits inside it).
LifeFlow_Logo_Overview.png   all six on one page.

Profile pictures can't move on Instagram or TikTok, so use a still there (for example Ray, or change it with the
season). The animated version is for video intros and outros, the website and the opening of the app.

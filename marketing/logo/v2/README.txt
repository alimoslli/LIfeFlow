The official LifeFlow logo: "Whoever's up", updated for the five skylings (Nov 11)

Same idea Ali chose on Nov 10: whichever skyling's time it is, peeking over the horizon, with that moment's real sky behind
it, all drawn by the app's own code. The cast is now the app's five: Dawn (first light), Ray (the day), Goldie (golden
hour), Twinkle (the evening) and Dreamy (late night). Noon and Dusk are retired, so the old files that show them are out
of date.

LifeFlow_Logo_Animated.mp4   1080 x 1080, 10 s loop, 30 fps, each skyling pops up for 2 s as the sky moves through a day.
LifeFlow_Logo_Animated.gif   540 x 540 version of the same loop.
LifeFlow_Logo_1_Dawn.png ... LifeFlow_Logo_5_Dreamy.png   the five stills, 1080 x 1080 (Ray works well as the profile picture).
LifeFlow_Logo_Overview.png   all five on one page.

Made from the app's own code on main dc32257 (bundle marketing/logo/entry.ts with esbuild into lf2.js, then
`node render_logo.cjs` here; CAST is the five at minutes 380, 560, 960, 1120, 1380 for a 6:35 AM to 4:50 PM day).

# "Meet the skylings" carousel (2026-11-10)

Eight feed slides (1080 x 1350): a cover, one slide per skyling, and "Which one is yours? Or let the sky choose." Skylings are
drawn by the app's sprite code and the skies are the app's colours at each skyling's moment (`lf2.js`, see `../../logo/`); every
word on a skyling's slide is the app's own from `src/lib/companions.ts` (moment, line, traits). Star is shown in its own night
colours with the moonlit rim. `render.cjs` serves the scratch folder and writes the PNGs.
Download (with the reel v2 and the logo intro and ending): https://d2ol7oe51mr4n9.cloudfront.net/user_303ATRwxPrvQJdn5jZHtWxZlmBa/4bccd51c-7511-489c-8213-962f53723d8d.zip

# LifeFlow words

## The one idea
LifeFlow gives you your time back, so you can spend it on your life.
Every script ends on what that time is for (people, dreams, rest, the thing you love), not on features.

## Say
| Say | Why |
|---|---|
| your skyling, or its name | the character on top of your day |
| your day, your sky | the app draws your day as the sky |
| makes room, gives you time back | what it does, said as the result |
| it noticed, it fixed it, you said yes | it acts first, you approve |
| the way you earn | covers every kind of work, not only shifts |
| Let's clear your sky. | the catchphrase |

## Never say
- assistant, AI assistant, personal assistant, companion, Jarvis, Dev
- productivity, planner, manage, organize, budget, all in one place, calm place
- safe to spend, daily allowance
- "Your life, connected." as a tagline or payoff
- "What can I take off your plate?"
- Rent (in ads)
- guilt words: slipping, you missed, falling behind
- "six skylings", Noon, Dusk, BudgetFlow
- that other apps "can't" connect or do something
- any number, review or testimonial that isn't real and checked

## Style
- Short sentences. Plain words. Talk to one person ("you").
- No em dashes anywhere a viewer sees. Use a period, comma or colon.
- Don't open with "AI". Open with a moment from someone's life.
- One script, one person, one moment. Across a campaign, show different lives: students, shift workers, nurses, creators, people running their own thing.

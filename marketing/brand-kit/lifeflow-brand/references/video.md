# How LifeFlow videos are made

## Format
- Vertical 1080 x 1920, H.264, AAC audio.
- App screen recordings: 30 fps. Camera footage: 24 fps with the shutter near 1/48 if the camera app allows it, exposure and focus locked.
- Keep text out of the top ~220 px and the bottom ~420 px (Instagram and TikTok cover those).

## Captions
- The LifeFlow caption box: Fraunces SemiBold, about 66 px tall at 1080 wide, white, centered, at most 2 lines and about 940 px wide.
- On a rounded navy box (`#0B1A3A` at 94%, corner radius about 44 px, soft shadow).
- The word "sky" is gold `#FFD27A`.
- Captions pop in quickly (a small overshoot) and fade out. See `assets/examples/caption-style.jpg`.
- Place captions where they don't cover the part of the app being shown.

## Structure that works for us
1. A hook in the first 2 seconds (a question, a real moment, or the skyling popping up).
2. The real app doing one thing, shown as it really happens.
3. The payoff is what the time is for (people, rest, dreams), not a list of features.
4. The logo ending: "Let's clear your sky."

## Filming people
- Real people with their own voices, talking like they're FaceTiming a friend. No reading.
- Film each moment at the time it happens (morning at 7 AM, night at 11 PM). The app draws the real sky for that time, so the phone in the shot matches the light around it.
- Real places only. No AI-generated backgrounds or AI sets.
- Telephoto (2x to 5x) from a few steps back looks more like film than the wide lens up close.
- When the phone screen is in the shot, show only the Today sky, never real bank balances or messages.

## Sound
- Music sits about 10 dB under voices and comes up in the gaps.
- Small pixel chimes on taps, stars and checkmarks. A short silence before a big reveal works.
- Master: -14 LUFS, true peak -2 dB.

## App footage
- Always record the current app with a test or demo account that has sample data.
- Never fake, mock up, or AI-generate an app screen.
- Show one action per shot: type under the sky, the answer lands on Today, tick it off, a star.

## Before posting
- Every number on screen must be real and checked (follower or view counts come from Instagram itself).
- Get Ali's OK on anything paid, or anything that names a price, partner or date.

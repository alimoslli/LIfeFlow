# What's true about LifeFlow today (November 2026)

Say only what's on this list. If you're unsure whether something is built, leave it out and ask Ali.

## Real and working
- Today is one screen: your day drawn as the sky, one note that matters, and a bar to type in.
- Type one sentence under the sky ("my paper's due tonight and I work 4 to 9") and the skyling finds the time and puts it on your day.
- Tick something off and you get a star. Stars and suns unlock things on your skyling's cloud.
- The five skylings follow the real sunrise and sunset where you live. Pick one and name it, or choose "Changes with the sky".
- Money: "Available now" (checking plus cash), spent and came in this month, bills, and "can I afford it" answered by the app's own math. Pay only counts once it's deposited. Money can be locked.
- School: Canvas classes and assignments from one link, what's due next.
- Goals (personal and money), promises ("I'll study tonight" gets checked back once), Health from Apple Health, Work shifts from a photo of the schedule.
- It asks before sending, spending or deleting anything.
- Strict coach on schoolwork: it helps you plan and study and never does graded work.
- Live on the web at lifeflowweb.com. The waitlist is open. The iPhone app is next.

## Built, but not open to everyone yet (don't show as live)
- Google Calendar and Gmail, Outlook and Teams calendars, bank connections through Plaid, texting the skyling.

## Not built (never claim)
- Apple Watch app, widgets, a "Tonight" card, calorie tracking.

## Prices
- $9.99 a month, $5.99 a month for students. Free while it's being tested.
- A free trial and a student offer are planned. Never state lengths, amounts, codes or dates.

## Privacy, in plain words
- Your data is locked to you, and what your skyling remembers is encrypted.
- Nothing trains on your data without your yes. Email, health and Google data never do.
- You can export or delete everything.

# LifeFlow brand basics

Start by looking at `assets/LifeFlow-brand-basics.png`: one page with the logo, the five skylings, the pixel clouds,
the colors and the fonts. Everything you make for LifeFlow uses these exact pieces.

The editing style, layouts, captions and pacing are **open**: the team is still improving them, so be creative there.
Only the basics below are fixed.

## The fixed basics
1. **Logo:** the app icon (a pixel sun rising over the water) and the "LifeFlow" name in Fraunces Bold.
   Files: `assets/logo/` (icon, lockups with white or navy text, "Let's clear your sky." text, and three logo videos:
   intro, ending and the "Whoever's up" loop). Never redraw, recolor or AI-generate the logo. Never the old blue sparkle
   logo or the old name BudgetFlow.
2. **Skylings:** the five characters, exactly as drawn by the app: Dawn (first light), Ray (through the day), Goldie
   (golden hour), Twinkle (into the evening), Dreamy (late at night). Stills in `assets/skylings/` (night versions of
   Twinkle and Dreamy for dark skies) and moving frames in `assets/skylings_animated/` (idle, wave, celebrate, think; one
   frame every 0.18 s, looped). Five only: never six, never Noon or Dusk. Never redraw, restyle or AI-generate them.
3. **Pixel clouds:** the app's own idea clouds in `assets/clouds/` (three shapes, day and night). Short text goes inside
   in navy. Stretch only the flat middle, never the puffs.
4. **Pixel art scaling:** skylings and clouds scale only in whole steps with no smoothing (nearest neighbor), so every
   pixel stays sharp.
5. **Fonts:** Fraunces for headlines, captions and the name; Geist for small text and anything that looks like the app
   (`assets/fonts/`, both free Google Fonts).
6. **Colors:** night navy `#142451`, moonlit blue `#152643`, gold `#FFD27A`, dawn peach `#E39568`, day sky `#487FC0`,
   horizon blue `#2F7CD2`, soft white `#F3F5FA`. More in `references/look.md`.
7. **App screens are real:** only real screen recordings or screenshots of the current app, from a test account with
   sample data. Never mock up or AI-generate an app screen.
8. **Words:** it's a **skyling**, never an "assistant", "companion" or "Jarvis". Don't lead with AI. The catchphrase is
   "Let's clear your sky." No em dashes on screen. Full list in `references/words.md`; what's true about the app today
   is in `references/app-facts.md` (never promise what isn't built, or a trial length, discount or date).

## Quick check before handing anything over
- [ ] The logo, skylings and clouds are the real files, crisp, unchanged
- [ ] Fonts are Fraunces and Geist; colors come from the palette
- [ ] "Skyling", never "assistant"; no em dashes; nothing promised that isn't true today
